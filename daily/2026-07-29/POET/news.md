  來源：[Siemens Semiconductor Packaging](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/semiconductor-packaging/2026/02/05/five-key-trends-of-co-packaged-optics-cpo-in-2026/) | [IDTechEx](https://www.idtechex.com/en/research-report/co-packaged-optics-cpo/1138) | [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260615-13098.html)
