# Investment plan — CEG as of 2026-05-15

## Crux of debate
1. **Multiple regime — AI-power growth (~22–25x) vs. utility re-rate (~17–18x).** With FY+1 EPS ~$13.60, the difference is roughly $245 (utility) vs. $299–$340 (growth). Both sides agree on the EPS anchor; they disagree on the exit multiple. Argus's downgrade plus PT trims ($400 → $370) and a fresh Sell are evidence the market is mid-re-rate, not finished.
2. **FERC large-load / colocation rule (Q2'26).** Front-of-meter MSFT TMI is immune, but the AMZN/GOOGL/META pipeline economics, and CEG's right to re-rate higher, hinge on the ruling. This is a dated, binary, falsifiable catalyst — the single biggest swing factor.
3. **Earnings-quality reversal sustainability.** 2025 OCF flipped from −$2.46B to +$4.24B and FCF turned positive ($1.29B), but FCF/NI is only 0.56, gross margin compressed 700bps, NI fell 38% YoY, and capex is stepping up. One year of clean cash flow after three years of derivative-collateral drag is not yet a trend.

## Where the bull is right
- Nuclear baseload is structurally unreproducible; 31.7 GW fleet is genuine scarcity and the V&E survey (96% cite power constraints) validates pricing power.
- MSFT TMI PPA is front-of-meter and FERC-immune — the flagship cash-flow story does not depend on the binary regulatory outcome.
- Balance sheet is a fortress: ND/EBITDA 0.9x vs. utility ~3–4x removes solvency tail risk and gives the stock a regulated-utility floor near $245 — limiting downside.
- 33% drawdown from ATH has already done most of the multiple-compression work; sell-side is raising Q1 estimates post-beat; institutional flow tilts positive (BlackRock +0.68%, FMR +8.38%).
- DOE $94M nuclear grant + 45U PTC are real, dated policy tailwinds.

## Where the bear is right
- Technicals are unambiguously broken: price below MA20/50/200, MACD histogram −3.99 and expanding, RSI 38.1 weak but not oversold, lower highs since Dec-2025, 12m relative underperformance ~3000bps vs S&P. No reversal signal yet.
- Earnings quality deteriorated in 2025: GM −700bps, NI −38%, tax rate doubled to 33.8%, $304M of negative unusuals, FCF/NI 0.56. Q1'26 revenue "doubling" is Calpine consolidation, not organic.
- Valuation premium is real: PEG 3.74 vs sector ~2.0; EV/EBITDA 15.25x vs utility 11x. The multiple already prices growth that hasn't arrived (TMI cash 2027–28).
- Zero insider open-market buying after a 33% drawdown is a damning non-signal; 80.1% institutional ownership leaves a thin marginal-buyer base.
- FERC Dec-2025 colocation rejection named CEG explicitly; the May 2026 large-load rule is unresolved and could tax behind-the-meter economics.

## Net verdict (3-5 sentences)
Both sides have credible, dated, sourced claims and the evidence quality is close — the bull owns the structural story (scarcity, balance sheet, FERC-immune flagship PPA, drawdown cushion), while the bear owns the near-term tape (broken technicals, accelerating MACD, multiple re-rate in progress, earnings-quality deterioration, regulatory binary). The fundamental floor (~$245 utility re-rate) is only ~11% below spot while the bull base ($299) is only ~9% above — risk/reward is roughly symmetric at $275 with a known binary catalyst ahead. The right posture is to wait: technicals signal more downside is likely before any setup completes (no oversold trigger, no reclaim of MA20/50 cluster $298–301), and the FERC ruling is the cleanest re-rate event. Initiating long here means buying into an active downtrend with no insider conviction underneath; initiating short here means fading a fortress balance sheet and a real structural thesis at a 33% drawdown.
