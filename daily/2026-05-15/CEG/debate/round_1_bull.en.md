## Moat
Nuclear baseload is structurally unreproducible: no new US nuclear plant has been built in a generation, and the AI build-out demands 24/7 carbon-free firm power that wind/solar/gas cannot match. CEG's ~31.7 GW nuclear capacity is the largest US fleet — supply curve is fixed, demand curve is vertical. Switching cost is infinite (you cannot relocate a reactor) and the V&E survey (96% of infra developers cite power constraints as the binding siting variable) validates pricing power. ROIC 17.1% vs. utility median ~6–8%.
