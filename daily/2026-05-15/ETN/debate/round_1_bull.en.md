# Bull case round 1 — ETN

## Thesis
ETN is the picks-and-shovels owner of AI-datacenter and US grid electrification — a multi-year, supply-constrained capex cycle that justifies a premium multiple with double-digit upside as backlog converts.

## Growth drivers
- Q1'26 Electrical Americas datacenter **orders +~240% YoY**, datacenter revenue **+~50% YoY** (news.md) — book-to-bill running well >1.
- Q1'26 revenue **$7.45B, +16.8% YoY** — strongest print in the series, re-acceleration confirmed (fundamentals.md).
- Operating margin **19.0%** in 2025 (+460bps over 3yr); FCF **$3.55B**, FCF margin 12.9% (fundamentals.md).
- US utility capex **+27% YoY to ~$1.4T through 2030**; AI share of US electricity 4% → 9% (news.md). Multi-year demand pull.
- Mgmt **raised 2026 organic growth + adj. EPS guide** citing AI datacenter demand (news.md).
- New **$30M / 370k sq ft MV switchgear plant** (Omaha) — capacity into the bottleneck.

## Moat
**Power-equipment lead times: standard transformers ~128 weeks, GSU ~144 weeks, HV substations 3–5 years** (news.md). Grain-oriented steel + copper constrained for years; new entrant capacity (Hitachi $1B, Siemens $421M) doesn't close the gap. Incumbents with installed code-compliant switchgear, utility relationships, and shippable capacity have **structural pricing power and switching-cost lock-in**. Scale: $27.4B revenue, 86.8% inst. ownership, deep utility/hyperscaler design-in.
