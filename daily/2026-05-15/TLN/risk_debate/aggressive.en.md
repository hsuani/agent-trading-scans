# Aggressive risk view — TLN

## Where trader is too cautious
- **Sizing leaves alpha on the table.** Medium (1.5% NAV) on a setup with R:R 4.1 → T1 and 11.2 → T2 is sub-optimal. Stop is only ~3% from spot; dollar risk per 1% NAV sized is ~0.045%. Going to 2.5% NAV still risks only ~0.11% NAV — a rounding error for an 11x asymmetric payoff. The plan even acknowledges "tight stop allows medium notional at small dollar risk."
- **Two-tranche scaling kills convexity around 5/18.** Splitting 60/40 and waiting for "MA200 reclaim with volume" means you only get the full clip *after* the catalyst tape has already paid you. The 5/18 PJM refile is asymmetric: bull read-through gaps the stock toward $372–$397 in one session and the 40% never gets filled at <$355.
- **Entry zone is fine; the discipline to enter NOW is right** — but the second tranche trigger is too conservative. Adding *only* on MA200 reclaim ($372) wastes ~$20 of edge vs adding on the 5/18 confirmation alone.
- **Options skew is being ignored.** 59% IV on a name with a hard binary catalyst 3 days out — June/July $360/$420 call spreads define dollar risk, give 3-4x payout on a T1 print, and let you keep equity sized normally. The current plan ignores this leveraged variant entirely.

## Recommended adjustments
- **Size: Medium 1.5% → Large 2.5% NAV** (rationale: dollar risk at $342 stop = ~0.11% NAV; well within any sane single-name risk budget for a 4.1:1 base-case R:R and verified Q1'26 print de-risking the guide).
- **Stop: $342 → $338** (give it room — ATR is $18.44, so a 3% stop is <0.6 ATR and will get whipsawed by intraday noise around 5/18; $338 is one ATR below MA50 and still above the $343.40 pivot only on a clean close basis. Dollar risk per share rises from $10.88 → $14.88, still <0.85x ATR).
- **Entry: full first tranche NOW $352.88; collapse the "MA200 reclaim" condition** — add the second tranche on the 5/18 read alone, don't wait for the chart to confirm what the tape already paid for.
- **Consider: leveraged variant** — TLN June 19 $360/$420 call spread for ~$15 debit (~0.3% NAV), max payout ~$60 (4x) if T1 prints by mid-June. Layer on top of equity, not instead of it.

## Asymmetry argument
Worst case max loss at upsized 2.5% NAV + $338 stop ≈ 2.5% × ($352.88−$338)/$352.88 = **~0.11% NAV**. Realistic upside to T1 ($397) = 2.5% × ($397−$352.88)/$352.88 = **~0.31% NAV**; to T2 ($475) = **~0.86% NAV**. Ratio T2 / max loss = **~7.8x at the portfolio level**, T1 / max loss = **~2.8x** — even after the upsize, the absolute dollar risk is trivial and the upside is meaningful.
