# Conservative risk view — VST

## Where trader is too aggressive
- **Stop on Setup A is $10/share wide** ($138.25 → $148.00 = 7.0%, ~1.4xATR above entry). With 4.9% ATR and 42.9% ann. vol, intraday whipsaw through $148 on any squeeze headline (short interest +33.6% MoM, 4.3% float short) is a coin flip. The proposal acknowledges squeeze risk but does not price it into stop placement.
- **Setup B T1 R:R = 1.4 is below the 1.5 threshold** and the rationale ("blended via 50% scale-out") understates the reality: stopping out before T1 is a full -$13.50/share loss against a +$14.50 expected gain on the runner half — fragile against 4.9% ATR.
- **Both setups ignore correlated power-IPP risk.** CEG broke down on a Q1 beat (news.md). TLN co-moves with VST. A long VST on MA20 reclaim has hidden 1.0+ beta to a CEG/TLN sector re-rate; one adverse hyperscaler PPA delay re-prices the cohort.
- **$4.23B current debt is a gap-risk catalyst not in either stop logic.** Refi announcement at Fed 3.50–3.75% prints overnight; stop is irrelevant.
- **"Wait for confirmation" is correct but Setup B's entry scale ($156–$159) front-runs a single MA20 close** — a one-day reclaim into a 35% downtrend has high failure rate without two closes above MA20 and MA50 reclaim.

## Tail scenarios
- **Scenario A (25%): Refi announcement at punitive spread** on $4.23B current debt → gap to $125–128 → Setup A short profits (+$10/sh on 0.5% NAV), Setup B long loses full stop $13.50/sh = -0.04% NAV. Manageable.
- **Scenario B (15%): Short squeeze on summer ERCOT heat headline** (4.3% float short, +33.6% MoM) → gap through $148 → Setup A stops out at $150–152 on open, slippage adds ~$3/sh → $14/sh loss = -0.045% NAV. Manageable but unpleasant.
- **Scenario C (10%): Sector contagion (CEG/TLN -8%)** drags VST -7% in a session → both setups whipsaw, false signals on both pivots.
- **Scenario D (8%): Cogentrix financing equity-issuance leak** → -10% gap, kills Setup B before it triggers.

## Recommended adjustments
- **Size: Small (0.5%) → Tiny (0.25% NAV) per leg.** Two pivots = potentially 1.0% gross exposure if both trigger sequentially; trim each leg to keep aggregate ≤0.5%.
- **Setup A stop: $148.00 → $145.00** (tighter, just above $142.34 shelf + 0.5xATR). Reduces $ loss per share from $9.75 to $6.75; lowers squeeze damage.
- **Setup B entry: require two consecutive closes > $155.71** AND MACD histogram contraction (not single close). Skip if volume < 10d avg.
- **Hedge consideration**: if Setup B triggers, pair with XLU short or short CEG vs VST long pair to neutralize sector re-rate risk.
