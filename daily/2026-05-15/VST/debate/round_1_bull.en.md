## Moat
Scarcity moat: dispatchable baseload + peaking generation interconnected to ERCOT/PJM cannot be replicated quickly — siting, permitting, and interconnection queues run 4–7 years. VST owns Comanche Peak nuclear (irreplaceable) plus a large gas fleet in the two highest-growth ISOs. 96% of infra developers are rerouting datacenter siting based on power availability (news.md 2026-05-14) — that is pricing power for whoever already has the megawatts. Retail arm (5M customers) provides natural hedge and earnings smoothing.
