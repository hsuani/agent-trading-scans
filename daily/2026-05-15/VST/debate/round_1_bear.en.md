# Bear case round 1 — VST

## Thesis (1 sentence)
Vistra is a leveraged merchant-power name whose AI-data-center re-rating is unraveling: FY25 net income halved, FCF collapsed 47%, net debt/EBITDA climbed to 3.81x, the chart is in a multi-month stair-step downtrend below all key MAs, insiders are net sellers with zero open-market buys, and the forward P/E of 12.6x only screens cheap if Street EPS nearly doubles — a bar that FY25's actual operating results do not support.

## Demand / market risks
- Despite the "AI power" headline, FY25 revenue grew only +3.0% YoY ($17.74B vs $17.22B) — the secular demand thesis is not yet visible in reported top-line.
- ERCOT/PJM load forecasts the bulls cite (5-6% / 2%+) are CEO guidance, not realized. Hyperscaler PPA pipeline (Cogentrix 5.5 GW, Meta nuclear) is multi-year — interim cashflow gap is real.
- Crowded trade: 91.6% institutional ownership, 102 hedge funds long, Pelosi-style retail attention. 4.3% of float short and short interest +33.6% MoM signals smart money rotating the other way.
- Peer CEG beat Q1 and still "undercut key level" per IBD — read-through: even good prints can't lift these names anymore.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- Gross margin compressed -10.8pp FY24→FY25 (43.7% → 32.9%). Operating margin halved (23.7% → 12.0%). EBITDA margin fell 40.4% → 28.5%.
- FCF: FY23 $3.78B → FY24 $2.49B → FY25 $1.32B (-47% YoY). TTM FCF only $477M per yfinance info.
- Capex $1.30B → $2.75B in three years (+112%), now eating 68% of OCF. CCGT build costs +66% since 2023 ($1,500/kW → $2,157/kW) — every new MW is more expensive, pressuring project IRRs.
- $228M FY25 impairments + -$175M unusual items — portfolio cleanup ongoing.
- Q1'26 op margin of 26.6% is a single-quarter snapback; FY25's full-year 12.0% is the structural baseline bears anchor on.

## Balance sheet risks
- Net debt $19.28B, up 21.6% YoY. Net debt/EBITDA 3.81x (was 2.28x end-FY24) — through the 3.5x preferred ceiling.
- D/E 355%. Tangible book value **negative** $-2.62B (goodwill+intangibles $5.25B mask thin common equity of $2.62B).
- Current ratio 0.78 (FY25 BS); quick ratio 0.26; working capital -$2.64B.
- $4.23B current debt rolling within 12mo, incl $1.23B revolver and $3.00B other current borrowings — refinancing risk into a Fed-on-hold environment (3.50-3.75%, only one cut projected 2026).
- FY25 debt issuance $6.02B vs $3.82B repaid — funding capex + buybacks with new debt, not earnings.

## Valuation risk

- Base bear: 10x × $9.00 = **$90, -37%**.

- P/B 18.3x and P/FCF on FY25 FCF ~36x are both stretched.

## Competitive / regulatory
