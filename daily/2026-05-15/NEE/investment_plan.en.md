- FY25 EPS $3.28 vs $3.37 (-2.7%) is a real first-decline data point despite +10.7% revenue — operating leverage broke this year.
- FCF/NI 0.47 means the $4.68B dividend is funded by debt issuance, not internal cash. Net debt +$22B over 3 years, $26B/yr gross issuance into a sticky long end is a tangible rate-path risk.

- 7/5/2026 IRA/OBBBA cliff is dated, binary, and outside management's control; tariff drag on imported solar/storage is a real IRR headwind under fixed-price PPAs.
- MACD just crossed bearish (0.61 vs 0.71) into 52w-high resistance ($98.75) — near-term risk/reward is asymmetric at this entry.

## Net verdict (3-5 sentences)
The bull case is grounded in two independently verifiable, dated catalysts (FPL settlement + named hyperscaler PPAs) with corroborating institutional flow and a clean uptrend. The bear case is built on real numbers (NI -1.6%, FCF/NI 0.47, premium multiples, 7/5 cliff) but its $69.70 / -27% target requires both a multiple compression to 17x AND an earnings haircut to $4.10 — a double-strike scenario that contradicts the safe-harbored backlog and signed PPA take-or-pay revenue. Net: thesis lands LONG with MEDIUM (not HIGH) conviction because (a) the stock is at 52w-high resistance with MACD rolling, (b) the 7/5 cliff creates a discrete event-risk window before Q2 earnings on 7/22, and (c) the entry offers limited upside to the $109 bull base (~+14%) vs ~-27% to the bear base — a 0.5x reward/risk that argues for partial sizing and a defined stop.
