# Neutral risk view — NEE

## Points of agreement (both sides)
- $89.17 is the correct technical hard-stop level (below 3/20 swing low) — neither side argues to move it lower.
- 7/5/2026 OBBBA cliff is a real, dated, binary event inside the 3-6mo horizon and deserves explicit treatment in sizing/structure.
- Entry at $95.68 is at-resistance (-3.1% from 52w high, MACD just-crossed bearish) — staged entry is preferable to a single-clip fill.
- A Q2 earnings print (7/22) is a second dated catalyst worth respecting in the structure.

## Aggressive overreach
- Where: Upsize to 2.5% NAV + 30% buy-at-market $95.68 + Sep'26 call spread overlay.
- Why: Concedes the 7/5 cliff is dated and binary, then adds size *into* the event window. The "ratio 3.9x asymmetric" math ignores Scenario A gap-through-stop (~12% slippage, not 6.8%) which the conservative documents from real bear-case mechanics. Buying at-market into a just-flipped MACD with price -3.1% from 52w high is also explicitly outside the trader's own Tier-1 plan ($89.90-$93.50). Vol-adjusting up because 34.5% IV is "low for the complex" still leaves NEE elevated for a regulated utility with D/E 157%.

## Conservative overreach
- Where: Cut to 0.75% NAV, tighten stop to $91.50, kill Tier-2 breakout entirely, defer adds until after 7/22.
- Why: A $91.50 stop sits *above* MA50 ($93.05 buffer is wrong direction) and below it only by $1.55 — that's 0.72x ATR14, well inside daily noise; it would whipsaw out on any ordinary $98.75 retest pullback. Killing Tier-2 entirely surrenders the upside-trigger path the investment plan explicitly defined (close >$98.75 = upgrade conviction). 0.75% on a Medium-conviction long with two named catalysts under-sizes the edge.

## Balanced adjustment proposal
- Size: **1.25% NAV pre-7/5; pre-authorized scale to 1.75% on confirmed >$98.75 close OR after 7/6 cliff guidance is benign.**
- Stop: **$89.17 hard, unchanged** (mental tighten to $91.00 only on Tier-2 breakout fill, since breakout invalidation is structurally different from pullback invalidation).
- Entry: **40/30/30** — 40% Tier-1 limits laddered $93.50/$92.00/$90.50; 30% on confirmed daily close >$98.75 with volume; 30% reserved for post-7/6 cliff clarity OR 7/22 earnings reaction.
- Hedge: **Aug $80 XLU puts sized to ~20% position delta** (cheaper, cleaner cliff/rate hedge than the aggressive's call spread, which adds same-direction premium decay into the event).
- Time horizon: **6-9 months** — through 7/22 earnings and into 2026 PPA prints.
