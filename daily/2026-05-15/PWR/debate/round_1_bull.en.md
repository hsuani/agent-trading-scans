# Bull case round 1 — PWR

## Thesis (1 sentence)
PWR is the scarce, skilled-labor-moated prime contractor monetizing a once-in-a-generation $1.3T US utility capex super-cycle, with a record $48.5B backlog, accelerating Q1 EPS beat (+35% to $2.68 vs $1.98 cons), raised FY26 guide ($13.55–$14.25 EPS), and confirmed peer read-through (MTZ +174% EPS) — justifying premium multiples for as long as bookings keep compounding.

## Growth drivers
- **Backlog explosion**: $48.5B record total backlog (Q1'26), broad-based across transmission, distribution, generation, load — not single-award concentrated; <25% of growth from the first meaningful 765 kV MSA. (news.md)
- **Utility capex tailwind**: S&P raised 2026-30 US utility capex forecast to ~$1.295T, +27% vs prior $1.1T; transmission capacity must expand 50-100% next decade, 4x by 2050 ($760B–$1.4T TAM). (news.md)
- **Revenue acceleration**: Q1'26 revenue $7.87B +26.3% YoY off larger base; FY25 $28.48B +20.3%; 3-yr CAGR ~18.6%. FY26 guide $34.7-35.2B (+22-24%). (fundamentals.md, news.md)
- **Pipeline optionality**: Mgmt flagged "none of CGT/generation announced is yet in backlog" pending air permits — future booking catalyst, not risk. Investor Day plan to nearly double mfg/fab/logistics footprint to capture hyperscaler modular DC demand. (news.md)
- **Margin expansion despite scale**: Gross margin 15.0% (+20bps), op margin 5.6% (+10bps), ROE 13.5% (+180bps) — operating leverage real. (fundamentals.md)

## Moat
Skilled-labor scarcity is PWR's structural moat — transmission permitting is 7-10 years, interconnection queues 5+ years (DOE/FERC). Utilities cannot self-perform 765 kV / HVDC at scale; PWR's union linemen, aviation fleet ($610M annual capex), and Cupertino Electric integration create reproducible execution that bolt-on peers (PRIM, MYRG) cannot match. Hyperscalers (Duke $102B, Southern $81B multi-year capex pledges directed customers) need PWR's full-stack EPC. FCF/NI 1.58x = high-quality earnings, not accrual.
