# Bear case round 1 — GEV

## Thesis (1 sentence)
GEV at $1090 is priced for flawless multi-year execution on AI-power capex (P/E 32 trailing / 44 fwd, EV/EBITDA 84, P/B 21, P/FCF ~79) while FY25 earnings were inflated by a $2.05B non-cash tax benefit, FY26 EPS is guided *below* TTM, Wind is hemorrhaging cash, a $4.4B Q1'26 special charge distorted reporting, and the chart shows the first bearish MACD cross after a 152% 12-month run — risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside.

## Demand / market risks
- **AI-capex single-point dependency**: The entire bull narrative rests on hyperscaler datacenter power buildout continuing at +$700B pace. Any AI-capex pause (model commoditization, NVDA airpocket, regulatory friction on power siting) collapses the order-growth narrative. Q1 Electrification booked $2.4B DC orders — one quarter > all 2025 — which is *exactly* the kind of pull-forward that mean-reverts.
- **Wind segment structurally broken**: Q1'26 Wind EBITDA **-$382M**, FY26 guide **-$400M**, plus **$225M restructuring**. Offshore ITC eligibility ended 1/1/2026 — Haliade-X pipeline impaired. OBBBA's foreign-entity material-assistance restriction tightens supply chain.
- **Tariff drag**: **$250–350M FY26 tariff hit** flagged for Wind/steel/electrical-steel; not a one-off if trade posture persists.
- **Backlog conversion risk**: $25.77B current deferred revenue (customer deposits) creates -$756M working capital — any order cancellation wave (gas slot deferrals, utility capex pause) hits cash directly.
- **Inventory build**: +$1.83B YoY ($7.82B → $9.64B) — if demand softens, write-downs follow.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- **Operating margin still tiny**: FY25 op margin **3.6%**, Q1'26 only **1.9%** (skewed by $4.4B special charge). Sector peers run 12–15%. The "supercycle" hasn't delivered industrial-grade margins yet.
- **FCF/NI 0.76** — below the healthy 0.9 bar even before normalizing for the tax gift.
- **Capex +45% YoY to $1.28B**; capacity expansion locks fixed cost into a cycle peak.
- **Wind losses + tariffs compound** to a >$600M FY26 segment drag before restructuring.

## Valuation risk
- **Bear**: P/E **22×** (sector median, Industrials Specialty Machinery) × FY26 fwd EPS **$24.76** = **$545** target → **-50%** from $1090.
- **Base bear**: P/E **28×** × $24.76 = **$693** → **-36%**.
- **Worst (multiple compression + EPS miss)**: 22× × $20 (Wind loss + tariff overshoot) = **$440** → **-60%**, matches 52w low $421.
- EV/EBITDA 84 vs sector 15-18 — even at 30× (premium) implies ~64% downside on EBITDA basis.
- **Forward P/E > Trailing P/E** is the tell: Street models EPS *contraction* in FY26 (TTM $34.24 → fwd $24.76).

## Competitive / regulatory
- **Siemens Energy €154B backlog, MHI doubling capacity into 2027–28** — the "oligopoly" is expanding supply. Pricing power is finite.
- **Ford Energy** new entrant in BESS; **Bloom, Vertiv** adjacent — fragmenting the AI-power TAM narrative.
- **Offshore Wind ITC sunset** (1/1/2026) is a permanent demand cliff for Haliade-X.
- **Tariff/trade policy** ongoing risk; steel/electrical-steel exposure unhedgeable.
- **BWRX-300 SMR** is years from revenue — option value, not earnings.

## Confidence

The trade is not "GEV is a bad company" — it's "GEV is priced for perfection at a cyclical peak with deteriorating quality of earnings."
