# Bear case round 1 — SO

## Thesis (1 sentence)
SO is a premium-multiple regulated utility funding an unprecedented $12.7B capex cycle with deepening negative FCF (-$2.94B), 5x net-debt/EBITDA, dilution, and net insider selling — leaving little margin for execution slip while the Georgia base-rate freeze caps base upside through 2028.

## Demand / market risks
- **Datacenter load is back-end-loaded and unrealized**: GA Power has ~11 GW under contract / 28 large-load projects, but conversion to billed kWh is multi-year. Hyperscaler PPAs can be cancelled, downsized, or delayed if AI capex resets (see broader hyperscaler scrutiny in 2026). The bull case implicitly extrapolates +12% GA Power sales growth through 2029 as a straight line.
- **Customer concentration risk emerges**: a few mega-hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta, AWS) will be increasingly material to GA Power's incremental revenue. Single-customer pullback could leave stranded capex on the rate base.
- **End-market saturation in residential/C&I**: ex-datacenter, weather-normal Southeast load growth is 1–2%. The narrative depends on one thin slice of demand.
- **Net income already fell -1.4% YoY in 2025 despite +10.6% revenue** — top-line growth is being absorbed by interest (+18%) and D&A (+14.5%). Operating leverage is *inverted*.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- **FCF -$2.94B in 2025 (vs +$0.83B 2024)** — FCF margin -9.9%. Capex jumped +42% YoY to $12.74B and management has guided continued elevated capex. Dividend ($3.02B paid) is funded entirely from debt + equity issuance.
- **Interest expense $3.24B (+18% YoY)** is a structural drag on EPS as the $74B debt stack rolls at higher rates. Every 100bps of refi cost ≈ $740M pretax pressure.
- **Operating margin compression**: 26.4% → 24.7% YoY. Gross margin -1.5pp. Vogtle units reportedly underperforming older units on availability (Inside Climate News May 2026).
- **Payout ratio 75.7%** on flat EPS limits dividend growth — yield 3.25% vs 5-yr avg 3.65% means investors are paying *more* for *less* yield cushion.

## Valuation risk
- **Bear**: peer-median forward P/E 17x × FY2026E EPS $4.92 = **$83.6**, **-10.8%** from $93.68.
- **Worst**: 15x × $4.70 (capex/regulatory haircut to consensus) = **$70.5**, **-24.7%**.
- EV/EBITDA 13.1x vs peer 11–12x; P/B 2.84x vs peer ~2.0x; PEG 2.66 — every relative-valuation lens flags premium.
- Beta 0.36: low absolute drawdown risk, but compression of the premium alone delivers double-digit downside without macro stress.

## Competitive / regulatory
- **Georgia PSC base-rate freeze through 2028** caps base-rate upside; recovery flows only through riders (storm $860M Helene, fuel) which are NOT margin-expanding.
- **Vogtle PR overhang**: 2-year-post-completion rate-bill pressure stories (Inside Climate News, Sierra Club) keep regulatory tone defensive heading into the 2028 rate case.
- **Clean-energy lag** vs NEE/VST flagged in retail chatter — risks IRP pushback as PSC pursues more renewables/storage.
- **Equity dilution**: 1.7% YTD 2026 share count growth; $1.62B issued in 2025 (vs $143M in 2024) — capex cycle requires recurring secondaries.
- **JPMorgan trimmed -29.8% in Q1 2026** — a sophisticated active manager voting against the premium.

## Rebuttal to bull (anticipated round 0)
- **Bull**: "DOE $26.5B loan derisks funding." Counter: it's a *loan*, not equity — it adds to the debt stack (already 5x ND/EBITDA, 73% debt/cap) and doesn't solve the FCF deficit; it merely lowers rate. Funding cheaper debt to fund negative-FCF capex is still leverage-up.
- **Bull**: "Q1 EPS beat ($1.32 adj vs $1.21)." Counter: GAAP EPS was $1.20 vs $1.21 — *flat*. Earnings growth +1.6% QoQ is rounding error; the beat is non-GAAP.
- **Bull**: "Datacenter load 42% YoY confirms thesis." Counter: off a small base; absolute MWh and revenue mix disclosure is thin; concentration risk in 3–4 hyperscalers.
- **Bull**: "Mizuho / Raymond James PT hikes." Counter: consensus is **15 Hold / 1 Sell / 1 Strong Sell vs 7 Buy** — Hold-leaning consensus drifting *more* defensive (Holds 13→15 over 3m). Two PT bumps don't override the 24-analyst tape.
- **Bull**: "25-yr dividend streak, defensive yield." Counter: 3.25% yield is **below** 5-yr avg 3.65% and **below** peer 3.8–4.0%; payout 75.7% on flat EPS = anemic dividend growth ahead.
- **Bull**: "Vogtle complete, derisked." Counter: completed reactors are reportedly *underperforming* older units; rate-bill backlash building ahead of 2028 case.
- **Bull**: "Institutional accumulation (BlackRock +2%, Capital World +37%)." Counter: passive flow. **25 insider sells, 0 buys** in 6mo from CEO/COO/CAO level — the people who know best are net sellers at $96–97. Active manager JPM trimmed -29.8%.
