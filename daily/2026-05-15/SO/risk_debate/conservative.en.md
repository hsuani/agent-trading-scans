# Conservative risk view — SO

## Where trader is too aggressive
- **Size 1.5–2% is too rich for a chase-into-resistance setup.** Price ($93.68) sits *below* MA20 and MA50, MACD hist negative, RSI 47, 1m return -1.01%. The trader is buying weakness inside a rolled-over short-term trend with no reclaim confirmation. The investment plan itself says "entries should be patient, not chased."
- **Stop $91.60 is the obvious level — and therefore a magnet.** Sits exactly at the 4/22 swing low; one wide-range day from spot is $1.53 ATR. A stop *at* the consensus level pre-shake-out risks getting tagged on noise without invalidating the long-term thesis.
- **Rate-sensitivity grossly underweighted.** SO is a long-duration bond proxy: $74B debt stack, 5.0x ND/EBITDA, 73% debt/cap, $3.24B interest expense (+18% YoY) already eating EPS. A +50bp 10Y move re-rates the multiple before fundamentals can respond. Trader does not stress this scenario.
- **Dilution drag is ongoing, not hypothetical.** Share count +1.7% YTD 2026; $1.62B equity issued in 2025 (vs $143M in 2024). Forward EPS estimates assume the issuance pace doesn't accelerate — but the DOE loan funds *some*, not all, of $12.74B capex.
- **Valuation premium (19x fwd, 13x EV/EBITDA, PEG 2.66) at top of utility band with insiders net sellers ($4–5M, no buys), JPM -29.8% trim, Hold-leaning consensus.**

## Tail scenarios
- **Scenario A — Rate shock (prob ~20%):** 10Y +40–60bp on hot CPI/Fed repricing → XLU -5–7%, SO -6–8% to $87–88 → MA200 fails → cascade to $83 (Dec '25 low). Hits stop at $91.60, ~$2.08/sh.
- **Scenario B — Hyperscaler PPA delay or GA PSC adverse ruling (prob ~15%):** DC load thesis cracks → multiple compresses to peer ~17x = $84. Stop hits.
- **Scenario C — Vogtle cost-recovery clawback or DOE loan condition tightens (prob ~8%):** Sharp gap-down through $91.60, slippage to $89 → loss ~$4.50/sh (2.2x intended risk).

- **Size:** Medium (1.5–2%) → **Small (0.75–1%)**. Add only on confirmation (MA50 reclaim on volume).
- **Stop:** $91.60 → **$91.20** (below the magnet level, accept slightly wider stop in exchange for fewer false flushes), OR keep $91.60 but size down so $ risk is identical.
- **Entry:** Don't take the full position at $93.68. **Wait for either (a) MA50 $95.39 reclaim with volume**, or **(b) successful retest of $92.00–$91.80 with intraday $93 reclaim.** Skip cold entry at current price.
- **Hedge:** Pair with **TLT short or 1-mo XLU put** (5% OTM) sized to 25% of position notional — cheap insurance against the rate-shock tail that dominates the loss distribution.

## Position-level $ risk
At 1.5% NAV, ~$93.40 avg entry, stop $91.60: risk = $1.80/sh × shares. For $100k NAV, position = $1,500 / $93.40 ≈ 16 sh → max loss ~$29 = 0.029% NAV. *Stated* risk is fine — but this assumes no gap. Scenario C gap-through to $89 doubles the loss to ~0.06% NAV. Acceptable in isolation, **not acceptable if also long XLU, NEE, AEP, or any other rate-sensitive utility** (correlated concentration). Verify sleeve exposure before sizing.
