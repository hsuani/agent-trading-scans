## Crux of debate
1. **Datacenter load durability**: Is GA Power's 28-project / ~11 GW pipeline and +42% YoY Q1 datacenter load growth a sustainable, ratebase-compounding stream, or a back-end-loaded, hyperscaler-concentrated narrative that can be downsized? Bull and bear both anchor to the same fact set; the bull has the regulator-approved framework (PPAs through PSC dockets) on its side, the bear has the legitimate "absolute MWh disclosure is thin" objection.
2. **DOE $26.5B loan — derisk or leverage-up?**: Bull frames it as cost-of-capital relief + equity-issuance overhang removal; bear frames it as more debt on a 5x ND/EBITDA stack. The bull has the stronger reading: a federally subsidized loan replacing market debt at lower coupon, with no incremental FCF stress and reduced dilution risk, is materially supportive.
3. **Valuation premium vs peers**: 19x fwd P/E and 13x EV/EBITDA vs peer 17-18x / 11-12x. Bear correctly flags the premium with PEG 2.66 and JPM trim; bull correctly notes the premium reflects Vogtle derisking + 12% GA Power load CAGR + DOE backstop that peers lack. Premium is justified but limits upside.

## Where the bull is right
- DOE loan is a genuine, dated, sourced structural positive (5/13/2026) — removes funding overhang and lowers refi cost on $74B debt stack.
- Datacenter thesis is real and confirmed by Q1 print (+42% YoY load, +25.5% est forward EPS growth to $4.92).
- Regulated monopoly + 2028 GA base-rate freeze + beta 0.36 = defensive floor; drawdown velocity is structurally muted.
- Sell-side rerate is active (RJ $104, Mizuho hike) and institutional accumulation (BlackRock +2%, Capital World +37%) outweighs JPM trim in dollar terms.

## Where the bear is right
- FCF -$2.94B and net debt $70.95B / 5.0x EBITDA leave no execution slack; dividend + capex funded by debt and equity issuance.
- 2025 net income -1.4% YoY despite +10.6% revenue — operating leverage is currently inverted as interest (+18%) and D&A (+14.5%) absorb top-line.
- Valuation premium of 10-15% to peer set is real; PEG 2.66; yield 3.25% vs 5-yr avg 3.65% = paying more for less.
- Hold-leaning consensus (15 Hold / 6 Buy / 1 SB / 1 Sell / 1 SS), insider selling (25 sells / 0 buys, ~$4-5M, clustered $96-97), and decelerating short-horizon technicals (price below MA20/MA50, MACD negative) argue against chasing.

## Net verdict (3-5 sentences)
The bull case has the higher-quality, dated, sourced evidence: a $26.5B DOE loan (5/13), a Q1 datacenter-driven adj EPS beat, ~11 GW contracted GA load, and a 2028 rate-freeze floor combine into a coherent multi-year ratebase compounder with limited drawdown velocity (beta 0.36). The bear's objections are valid but largely structural-to-utility (negative FCF during capex cycles, 5x leverage at top of comfort range, dilution) — they cap upside and warrant smaller sizing, not avoidance. Net: LONG bias with sized humility — the asymmetry favors longs given the 2028 regulatory floor, but the valuation premium and decelerating near-term technicals mean entries should be patient, not chased. Target: bull base $98-103 (12mo), downside risk $87-91 if MA50 fails and $91.60 breaks.
