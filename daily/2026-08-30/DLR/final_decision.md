## Macro Assessment

**AI training demand**: DLR's large wholesale data centers are the natural fit for GPU training clusters requiring hundreds of MW of power. Major AI companies are signing long-term (10-15 year) power-take contracts.

**Wholesale pricing risk**: Unlike EQIX (interconnection moat), DLR's wholesale pricing faces competition from hyperscalers building their own facilities. Long-term, some demand may be cannibalized.

**Balance sheet**: DLR has been working to reduce leverage after 2022 asset disposals. Net Debt/EBITDA ~7-8x remains elevated.

## Decision

**Direction:** HOLD (2% NAV)  
**Conviction:** 4/10

### Rationale
DLR is a solid #2 data center REIT with genuine AI demand tailwind. Lower quality than EQIX (no interconnection moat) but trades at lower valuation, providing some margin of safety. The wholesale model faces long-term competitive pressure from self-builds, limiting upside.

### Conditions to Add
- Major AI company announces 200MW+ contract with DLR
- Balance sheet improves (Net Debt/EBITDA <6x)
- P/AFFO drops below 18x on sector weakness

### Conditions to Reduce
- Hyperscaler self-build announcements accelerate materially
- Balance sheet worsens (Net Debt/EBITDA >9x)
- Lease signings disappoint for 2+ consecutive quarters
