## Macro Assessment

**Unique transformation story**: IRM is converting from a slow-growth records storage business into a high-growth data center operator. The legacy business provides stable cash flows to fund data center expansion without dilutive equity raises.

**AI data opportunity**: IRM's vast archive of historical documents (80%+ of Fortune 1000 companies) could be digitized and licensed as AI training data — a potentially large but speculative opportunity.

**Execution**: Matterhorn is aggressive. Adding 500MW+ of data center capacity requires significant capital and execution discipline. Track record so far has been positive.

## Decision

**Direction:** HOLD (2% NAV — transformation play)  
**Conviction:** 4/10

### Rationale
IRM offers a differentiated story vs pure-play data center REITs. The legacy business provides a stable foundation while digital accelerates. At similar valuation to DLR but with more upside optionality from the transformation and AI data opportunity. Execution risk is the main concern.

### Conditions to Add
- Project Matterhorn leasing pace accelerates (>100MW signed/quarter)
- AI data licensing revenue materializes from legacy archives
- Digital revenue exceeds 50% of total company AFFO

### Conditions to Reduce
- Data center AFFO growth disappoints (<15% YoY) for 2+ quarters
- Legacy business declines faster than digital replaces it
- Matterhorn capex overruns materially
