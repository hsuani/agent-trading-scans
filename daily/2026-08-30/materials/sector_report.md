## Detailed Rankings

### #1 FCX — Copper is the Metal of the Energy Transition
Copper underpins virtually every clean energy technology: EVs, solar, wind, data centers, grid upgrades. FCX is the world's most liquid copper pure-play with world-class assets and decades of reserve life. At P/E 12-15x (copper price dependent), valuation is reasonable vs other materials.

**Key watch**: Q3/Q4 copper demand data from China infrastructure + global EV sales pace.

### #2 LIN — Quality Compounder
Linde is the highest-quality industrial company globally. The semiconductor fab buildout is a genuine demand driver. While organic growth (3-5%) doesn't qualify for active trading, LIN is the appropriate quality anchor in any materials allocation. Premium valuation reflects deserved quality.

**Key watch**: Semiconductor fab utilization rates; hydrogen contract wins.

### #3 APD — Hydrogen Option
Air Products carries the highest hydrogen infrastructure risk/reward. NEOM and US Hydrogen Hubs are transformative projects — if executed. The long waiting period and execution risk justify only a small speculative position. New CEO strategy clarity would be a major catalyst.

**Key watch**: NEOM production start milestone; 45V hydrogen tax credit regulations.

### #4 MP — NdPr Recovery Required
MP Materials has genuine strategic value but is uninvestable until NdPr prices recover materially. The ~70% price decline has destroyed near-term economics. DoD contracts are insufficient to restore profitability at scale. Wait for price signal.

**Trigger**: NdPr spot >$80/kg sustained + magnet factory completion.

### #5 ALB — Lithium Cycle Bottom Unknown
Albemarle has world-class lithium assets but the supply glut makes near-term fundamentals untenable. Lithium prices would need to roughly double from current levels to restore ALB to previous earnings power. Entering before the cycle turn risks continued capital erosion.

**Trigger**: Lithium carbonate >$25k/t sustained + China EV demand surge confirmation.
