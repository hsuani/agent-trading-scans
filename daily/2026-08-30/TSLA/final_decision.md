## Decision

**Direction:** HOLD (1% NAV — speculative option on Optimus/FSD)
**Conviction:** 3/10

### Rationale
TSLA fails the valuation gate decisively (P/E >100x). The bull case requires Optimus production at scale (100,000+ units/year) and Robotaxi commercial operation — both unproven. At current valuations, the stock prices in a perfect execution of multiple optionalities simultaneously.

However, Tesla holds unique assets: the world's largest real-world driving dataset (FSD training), the most advanced humanoid robot in early production (Optimus), and a global charging/service network. These are hard to replicate and justify a small speculative position.

### Conditions to Add
- Optimus production reaches 10,000+ units/quarter with confirmed external customer orders
- Robotaxi revenue materializes at >$100M run rate
- EV gross margin stabilizes above 18%

### Conditions to Reduce / Sell
- Optimus production delays extend beyond 2027
- Elon Musk divestiture of Tesla stake (management risk signal)
- Core EV market share falls below 15% globally

## Final Verdict: HOLD (1% NAV speculative)
