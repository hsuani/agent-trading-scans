## Macro Assessment

**Tower REIT model**: AMT's business is extremely stable — escalating lease contracts with US major carriers that renew automatically. Each tower hosts 2-4 tenants; adding a tenant is nearly pure profit (marginal cost ~10% vs full revenue).

**5G densification**: As 5G rollout moves from macro sites to small cells and repeaters, tower and rooftop demand increases. AMT benefits from more spectrum bands per tower.

**International diversification**: India (Jio/Airtel 5G) and Africa are high-growth markets. Currency risk is the offset.

**Growth headwinds**: T-Mobile-Sprint merger created tenant consolidation (fewer tenants per tower in some markets). 5G buildout by US carriers is maturing. Organic growth is slowing to ~6-8% AFFO/share.

## Decision

**Direction:** HOLD (2% NAV — stable yield + infrastructure)  
**Conviction:** 3/10

### Rationale
AMT is a high-quality, defensive REIT with predictable cash flows and infrastructure moat. However, it is an inferior investment to data center REITs in the current AI capex environment. AFFO growth of 6-8% is below the sector leaders. At P/AFFO ~20-22x, the price is fair but not compelling. Hold as a defensive infrastructure anchor rather than a growth play.

### Conditions to Add
- US 5G capex cycle restarts with new spectrum deployment (e.g., millimeter wave)
- India operations accelerate beyond 20% AFFO contribution
- P/AFFO drops below 18x (better entry opportunity)

### Conditions to Reduce
- T-Mobile or Verizon announces material tower portfolio reduction
- US organic growth falls below 4% AFFO/share
- Interest rates rise above 5% again (high-leverage REIT valuation compression)
