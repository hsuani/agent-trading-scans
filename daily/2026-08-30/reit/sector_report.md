## EQIX vs DLR: Colocation vs Wholesale

**EQIX advantage**: Interconnection moat. 9,000+ networks and cloud providers connected at EQIX IBX locations — a network effect that compounds over time. This is not a commodity. AI companies need EQIX for low-latency routing between GPU clusters and distributed applications.

**DLR niche**: AI training clusters require massive power blocks (50-500MW) — DLR's specialty. But this is more commoditized; hyperscalers can self-build. DLR's moat is speed-to-market and global scale, not interconnection.

**Verdict**: EQIX is superior quality at similar valuation. Both warrant allocation; EQIX gets larger share.

## IRM: The Wild Card

IRM is misunderstood by many investors who see it as a slow-growth legacy records business. The transformation to data centers (Project Matterhorn) + potential AI data licensing from decades of archived Fortune 1000 records is a compelling optionality trade. At similar P/AFFO to DLR but with higher optionality, IRM deserves equivalent weight.

## AMT: Stability Premium

AMT is the most defensive and least correlated to AI demand among the four. Tower REITs benefit from 5G densification and emerging market growth (India, Africa). However, slowing US organic growth and the maturing T-Mobile integration make AMT the lowest-conviction hold in this group.
