## Macro Assessment

**Growth constraint**: Industrial gases is a mature oligopoly. Organic growth is 3-5%/year. Linde's P/E reflects quality, not growth. At 30-35x, the stock is priced for perfection.

**Dividend compounding**: For long-term portfolio investors, LIN is a dividend compounder. For active trading, the upside is limited.
