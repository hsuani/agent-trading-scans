## Macro Framework Assessment

### Bullish Factors (for bond prices / long duration)
- Fed rate cut cycle (2024–2025): each cut → TLT price appreciation
- Recession risk: if US GDP slows sharply → flight to treasuries
- Disinflation if PCE continues falling toward 2% target
- Safe-haven demand in risk-off environments

### Bearish / Risk Factors
- Sticky inflation (PCE ~2.5–3% as of 2025): limits how far Fed can cut
- Fiscal deficit ($1.8T+/year): massive treasury supply → upward pressure on long yields
- Term premium re-expansion: investors demand more compensation for duration risk
- Economic soft-landing scenario → less urgency for rate cuts → long yields stable or higher

### Key Rate Dynamic
- Short rates: Fed-controlled → declining (bullish for TLT via carry)
- Long rates (10Y, 30Y): market-driven → fiscal deficit and term premium push HIGHER
- Result: Yield curve steepening → TLT underperforms short-duration treasuries

### Net Macro Score: UNCERTAIN / SLIGHT BEARISH LEAN (for long duration)
