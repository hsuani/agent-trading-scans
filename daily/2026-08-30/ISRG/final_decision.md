## Decision

**Direction:** HOLD (2% NAV — highest quality robotics holding)
**Conviction:** 5/10

### Rationale
ISRG is the highest-quality robotics business in the sector with genuine, proven competitive moats (installed base network effects, surgeon training, hospital integration). Revenue growth ~15% with 70%+ gross margins and $7-8B net cash is exceptional. The only reason this doesn't qualify for full pipeline is the P/E ~60-70x which fails the valuation gate — but this is a quality premium worth paying.

### Conditions to Add
- P/E compresses to <50x (market correction → strong buy)
- da Vinci 5 international regulatory approvals accelerate
- Ion revenue becomes material (>15% of total procedures)

### Conditions to Reduce
- Competition from Medtronic Hugo or J&J Ottava gains >5% US market share
- Procedure growth decelerates below 10% YoY for 2+ quarters
- P/E expands above 80x (excessive speculation)

## Final Verdict: HOLD (2% NAV — best quality robotics)
