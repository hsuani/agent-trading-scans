## Macro Assessment

**Best-in-class**: Equinix has the most defensible moat in data center REITs through its interconnection platform. Network effects (every carrier, cloud, and enterprise connecting at EQIX IBX locations) create a virtuous cycle that competitors cannot replicate cheaply.

**Rate sensitivity**: REITs suffered significantly in 2022-2023 rate rise cycle. Fed cutting is a structural positive for REIT valuations.
