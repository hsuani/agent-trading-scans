## Detailed Assessment

### 1. GLD — Best Macro Fit
**Rationale**: Gold's structural bull market is driven by central bank demand (1,000+ tonnes/year), Fed cutting cycle, geopolitical risk, and de-dollarization. These tailwinds are multi-year and not sensitive to any single quarter's data. Gold is the only hedge instrument with clearly positive asymmetry in the current regime.

**Risk**: Short-term, if USD strengthens materially or real rates rise, gold faces headwinds. But these are tactical, not structural.

**Allocation**: 2–5% NAV core position. Accumulate on USD-driven dips.

### 2. UUP — Conditional Hedge
**Rationale**: If portfolio carries significant Asian/TW equity exposure (which this system does for tw_photonics, tw_probe), a USD hedge makes conceptual sense. UUP provides USD exposure with 57.6% EUR weight. Rate differential (Fed > ECB/BOJ cutting) provides near-term support.

**Risk**: Futures roll decay, de-dollarization structural trend, and high expense ratio (0.75%) make UUP an expensive hedge. Direct currency hedging is more efficient for large exposures.

**Allocation**: 0–1% NAV only if specific FX hedge is needed.

### 3. TLT — Wait for Better Entry
**Rationale**: Long-duration treasuries are a classic recession hedge and would benefit from deep Fed cuts. However, the fiscal deficit creates persistent supply pressure on the long end, causing curve steepening. TLT (17-year duration) has significant mark-to-market risk if 10Y yields rise from current levels.

**Better Alternative**: SHY (1-3Y) or IEF (7-10Y) for duration exposure with less fiscal risk.

**Allocation**: 0–1% NAV maximum; wait for 10Y above 5.5% or confirmed recession.

### 4. SH — Avoid
**Rationale**: SH's daily reset mechanism means it is structurally wealth-destroying in a trending bull market. The S&P 500 has been in a sustained uptrend since 2022 lows, and Fed cutting cycle + AI capex super-cycle suggest continuation. SH should only be used for 1-4 week tactical hedges around specific event risks, never as a portfolio holding.

**Allocation**: 0% currently. Would consider only with a specific 2-4 week catalyst (Fed hawkish surprise, major geopolitical shock).

## Pair / Strategy Notes
- **Gold vs Inverse Equity**: GLD and SH are not substitutes. GLD provides long-term protection; SH is a short-term tactical tool. Do not confuse their roles.
- **Rate sensitivity**: If Fed signals pause or hike → GLD under pressure; TLT also hit. Both hedges would fail simultaneously in a rate-shock scenario.
- **Geopolitical escalation**: GLD strongest beneficiary; TLT secondary safe-haven. SH and UUP reaction depends on risk-off intensity.
