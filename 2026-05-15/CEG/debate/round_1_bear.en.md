# Bear case round 1 — CEG

## Thesis (1 sentence)
CEG is a structurally levered IPP being repriced from an AI-power growth name back toward a utility multiple while earnings quality, technicals, regulation, and integration risk all deteriorate simultaneously — a falling knife at $275 with no insider conviction underneath.

## Demand / market risks
- **AI-power thesis is the entire premium, and it is contested.** Argus already downgraded arguing CEG belongs at a traditional utility multiple, not an AI-premium growth multiple — that re-rate alone closes most of the gap to fair-value (analysts have already trimmed fair value from ~$400 to ~$370, and the high-low PT spread $441 vs $272.40 shows zero consensus).
- **MSFT TMI is a 2027–2028 cash flow** — restart is multi-year execution; any NRC, fuel-supply, or labor slip pushes economics out and exposes the stock to a multi-year air-pocket where the premium has been pre-paid but cash hasn't arrived.
- **PPA pipeline beyond MSFT is hopeful, not contracted.** AMZN/GOOGL/META talks are "in negotiation per industry reporting" — not signed.
- **Hyperscaler capex is cyclical.** A single guide-down cycle from MSFT/META/GOOGL collapses the scarcity narrative; CEG has beta 1.155 vs utility 0.5 — it will trade like a tech derivative on the way down.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- **Gross margin collapsed 700bps YoY** (25.4% → 18.4%); Q1'25 hit 12.7%. Fuel/cost-of-revenue grew faster than revenue.
- **Net income −38% YoY** ($3.75B → $2.32B) despite +8.3% revenue growth — the operating leverage is going the wrong way. Tax rate doubled (17.1% → 33.8%) and $304M of negative unusuals appeared.
- **FCF/NI = 0.56**, well below the 0.9 healthy threshold; 2022–2024 saw three consecutive years of negative operating cash flow on derivative collateral — one cycle reversal does not prove sustainability.
- **Capex stepping up** ($2.42B → $2.57B → $2.95B) for TMI restart + Calpine integration; buybacks already cut in half ($999M → $400M) to fund it.

## Valuation risk
- Bear: P/E 18× (utility re-rate per Argus) × FY+1 EPS ~$13.62 (implied by current $275.26 / 20.21x fwd) = **~$245**, **−11% from $275**.
- Worst: P/E 15× × bear EPS $12.50 (Calpine integration drag, gross-margin slip) = **~$188, −32%** — back to the 52w low zone $243 and below.
- PEG 3.74 vs sector ~2.0 is the cleanest tell: even granting bull growth, the multiple already prices it.

## Competitive / regulatory
- **FERC PJM colocation overhang is the dominant binary.** Dec 2025 FERC 2-1 rejected the Amazon-Talen amended ISA and explicitly named CEG among affected IPPs — direct nuclear-to-data-center plug-in deals are blocked, forcing front-of-meter PPAs at worse economics.
- **Pending FERC large-load (>20MW) rulemaking** could codify grid-cost reallocation to behind-the-meter loads — that taxes the entire AI-power thesis.
- **TMI Crane restart is execution risk**: first U.S. nuclear restart of its kind; NRC licensing, supply chain, workforce — any of which can slip and re-price the 20yr $16B PPA NPV.
- **Calpine $30B is dilutive scale, not synergy.** Issued 50M shares + $4.5B cash; debt rose $580M in 2025; integration costs flagged in Q1 8-K caused the initial sell-off. ShareholderRightsRisk = 9/10.
- **Nuclear PTC floor is a regulatory floor, not a ceiling** — it caps downside, doesn't justify 23.9x P/E.

- Bull claim: "Q1 beat, $13B FCF outlook reaffirmed."
  Counter: Q1 doubled revenue **only because Calpine closed Jan 2026** — apples-to-oranges; the same release flagged integration costs and triggered the 6%+ slump on 2026-05-13.
- Bull claim: "DOE $94M grant + nuclear PTC tailwind."
  Counter: $94M is rounding error on $99B mkt cap; PTC is a floor, already in numbers.
