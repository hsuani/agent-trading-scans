# Neutral risk view — VST

## Points of agreement (both sides)
- Two-pivot structure is correct: no engagement at $141.90; asymmetry sits at the $138 break or $155.71 reclaim.
- Trigger-based entry (no pre-positioning) is the right discipline given 42.9% ann. vol.
- Squeeze risk on Setup A is real (SI +33.6% MoM, 4.3% float) and must be priced — disagreement is *how* (size vs stop).
- $4.23B refi overhang is a gap risk no stop can defend; activity should pause around any refi headline and into Q2'26 print.
- Volume confirmation (>10d avg 5.74M) is required on either trigger.

## Aggressive overreach
- Where: upsizing to 1.25% NAV and adding a 0.25% June/July $160/$185 call spread overlay on Setup B.
- Why: plan explicitly calls "half-size or smaller" at MEDIUM conviction with bifurcated cruxes (8/10 confidence both ways). 1.25% on a 42.9%-vol name with a 7% stop is ~3% NAV impact on a single ATR overshoot; the gap-risk catalyst ($4.23B current debt at Fed 3.50–3.75%) is not priced into a 1.25% sizing. Call-spread overlay layers gamma on top of a directional thesis that hasn't yet earned a core position — premium decay if MA20 reclaim fails to follow through is real, and squeeze convexity is already partially captured by trigger-only common exposure.

## Conservative overreach
- Where: cutting to 0.25% per leg AND tightening Setup A stop to $145 AND requiring two-close confirmation AND XLU/CEG sector hedge.
- Why: this is four independent risk reducers stacked, which collectively neuter the trade. Tightening stop to $145 (0.5xATR above the $142.34 shelf) puts the stop inside the noise band of a 4.9% ATR — a routine bounce off $142.34 stops out the short before the move develops. Two-close Setup B confirmation surrenders 3–5% of the squeeze move (exactly what makes the bull leg asymmetric). Sector hedge adds basis risk and execution cost on a tactical 1–4w trade. Pick one or two, not all four.

## Balanced adjustment proposal
- Size: **Small 0.75% NAV per leg** (compromise: above conservative's 0.25%, below aggressive's 1.25%; sized to absorb one stop-out without dominating monthly P&L). Cap aggregate gross exposure at 1.0% if both trigger sequentially — trim second leg to 0.25%.
- Stop: **Setup A $147 (tightened from $148 by $1 to reduce squeeze damage but preserve room above $142.34 shelf + 1xATR); Setup B $144 unchanged.**
- Entry: keep trigger-based with resting stop-orders (aggressive is right that manual-entry-on-confirmation forfeits the first $2–3). Resting stop-sell $137.90 / stop-buy $156.20. Require volume > 10d avg on the trigger bar; one-close on Setup B is acceptable IF accompanied by MACD histogram contraction and volume confirmation.
- Hedge: **skip standing sector hedge.** If Setup A AND Setup B both trigger within 5 sessions (whipsaw regime), reduce both to 0.375%. No call-spread overlay until/unless Setup B holds T1 ($172) — then a runner-only spread is defensible.
- Time horizon: 1–4w tactical; flatten 48h before Q2'26 print (early August) and around any refi announcement.
