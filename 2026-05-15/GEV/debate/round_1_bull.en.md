# Bull case round 1 — GEV

## Thesis (1 sentence)
GEV is a sold-out, oligopoly-priced power-equipment compounder riding a multi-year AI-datacenter + grid-replacement supercycle with a fortress balance sheet, accelerating FCF, and a raised FY26 guide that the Street is still under-modeling.

## Growth drivers
- **Gas turbine backlog 83 → 100 GW in one quarter**, targeting 110 GW + slot reservations by YE26; total backlog $163B trending toward $200B ahead of schedule (news.md). Slots are now booked into 2028+ industry-wide.
- **Electrification Q1 datacenter orders $2.4B in a single quarter — more than full-year 2025** (news.md). Datacenter critical power tracking 96 GW global by EOY26; GEV is a gatekeeper SKU (transformers, switchgear, HV grid).
- **Q1'26 orders $18.3B, +71% YoY**; FY26 guide raised to revenue $44.5–45.5B, EBITDA margin 12–14%, FCF $6.5–7.5B (news.md). Implies FCF nearly doubles again off the FY25 $3.71B base.
- **Operating leverage**: gross margin 11.7% → 14.5% → 17.4% → 19.8% (FY22-25), ~250-300bp/yr (fundamentals.md). Wind drag (-$382M Q1) is the *last* offset; legacy offshore book rolls off.
- **FCF +118% YoY to $3.71B in FY25; capex +45% YoY to $1.28B** funding capacity for the locked-in backlog (fundamentals.md).

## Moat
Three-player global heavy-duty gas turbine oligopoly (GEV / SIEGY / MHI). SIEGY backlog €154B record, MHI slots sold into 2027-28, both expanding capacity but unable to meet demand (news.md). That is **structural pricing power**: customers cannot multi-source, switching costs are 30-year service contracts, and the installed-base services annuity is captive. Add HV transformer scarcity and AI-grade grid software (Electrification) and GEV controls a chokepoint, not a commodity.

## Upside scenario
FY26 guide midpoint: revenue $45B, EBITDA margin 13% → EBITDA ~$5.85B; FCF midpoint $7B.
- **Base**: 50× FY27E EPS ~$28 (Street fwd $24.76 + operating-leverage uplift as Wind loss narrows) → **$1,400**, +28% from $1,090.
- **Bull**: Argus PT $1,300 reachable on FY26 print alone; on 60× × $28 = **$1,680**, +54%. If backlog hits $200B and 2027 EPS trajectory rerates to $34, 50× = **$1,700+**.
- **Valuation defense**: P/FCF ~79 today drops to ~42 on FY26 FCF $7B at unchanged price — i.e. the multiple compresses *via earnings, not price*. EV/EBITDA 84 collapses to ~50 on FY26 EBITDA $5.85B and to ~30 on FY27 run-rate. The "rich" multiple is a snapshot artifact of pre-inflection EBITDA.
