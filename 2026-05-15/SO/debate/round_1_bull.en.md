# Bull case round 1 — SO

## Thesis (1 sentence)
Southern Company is the lowest-risk, regulator-blessed way to own the Southeast AI/datacenter power supercycle: a $26.5B DOE loan just de-risked the largest capex cycle in its history, Q1 datacenter load grew +42% YoY, and the 2028 base-rate freeze plus beta 0.36 cap downside while EPS compounds at a regulator-approved ~5–7% clip into a structurally tightening power market.

## Growth drivers
- **Datacenter load surge**: Q1 2026 datacenter electricity usage +42% YoY drove the EPS beat ($1.32 adj vs $1.21 prior); GA Power has 28 large-load projects ~11GW under contract (news.md).
- **Regional load growth**: ~10GW contracted load growth across the region in 2026; 8% annual sales growth through 2029, **12% at Georgia Power** — multi-year rate-base compounder (news.md).
- **DOE $26.5B loan (5/13/2026)**: backs >16GW of GA/AL capacity build; slashes cost of capital on a $74B debt stack and removes equity-issuance overhang (news.md).
- **Vogtle 3&4 in-service**: nuclear base now earning, derisked vs construction era; CEG/TLN nuclear premium reads across.
- **Revenue reacceleration**: FY25 revenue $29.55B, +10.6% YoY; forward EPS $4.92 = +25.5% est growth (fundamentals.md).

## Moat
Regulated monopoly franchises (Georgia Power, Alabama Power, gas distribution) with state-PSC-set ROE on rate base. 24/7 firm baseload + new nuclear = the exact power profile hyperscalers need and cannot self-build. Switching cost for a datacenter sited in GA is effectively infinite. 25-year dividend-increase streak signals institutional capital discipline.
