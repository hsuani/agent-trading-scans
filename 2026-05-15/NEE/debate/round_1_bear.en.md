# Bear case round 1 — NEE

## Thesis (1 sentence)
NEE is a capital-trapped, 157% D/E renewables compounder trading at a regulated-utility premium (fwd P/E 21.8x vs peer 17-19x) into a tightening rate, policy, and macro backdrop — IRA/OBBBA's 2026-07-05 begin-construction cliff, refinancing of a $95.6B debt stack, and -1.6% FY25 NI on +10.7% revenue expose the "AI power" multiple as fragile, with MACD already rolling negative near the 52w high.

## Demand / market risks
- Renewables tail risk is binary, not gradual: OBBBA forces wind/solar projects to begin construction before **2026-07-05** or be in service by 2028-01-01 to keep ITC/PTC. Post-cliff NEER pipeline beyond safe-harbored backlog is materially impaired; domestic-content threshold rises to 50% and material-assistance rules apply to projects starting after 2025-12-31 — compliance friction + cost-pass-through limited under fixed-price PPAs.
- Trump tariff drag on imported solar modules, inverters, batteries — squeezes NEER project IRRs on already-signed PPAs (take-or-pay = NEE eats input inflation).
- Hyperscaler PPA narrative (Meta 2.5 GW, Google multi-GW) is priced in (+36.6% 12m); incremental deals must clear a high bar to move the stock. Datacenter power-demand thesis is the consensus trade across CEG/TLN/VST/PPL — relative attractiveness eroding.
- FPL rate case approval actually *cut* the 2026 revenue request 39% ($1.545B → $945M) and 2027 by 17% — bull framing as "visibility" obscures that regulators clipped ~$600M of asked-for recovery.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- FY25 net income **-1.6%** to $6.84B despite +10.7% revenue — operating leverage broke; D&A $6.94B and interest expense absorbing growth. EPS $3.28 (2025) vs $3.37 (2024) = first EPS decline.
- Reported FCF $3.21B on $6.84B NI = **FCF/NI 0.47**; yfinance TTM FCF -$18.5B inclusive of growth capex. Either way, dividend ($4.68B) is **not** covered by FCF — funded by debt issuance.
- Capex locked at ~$9.3B/yr to feed FPL rate base + NEER backlog — no discretion to deleverage. Working capital persistently -$9.2B.
- Current ratio **0.54**, quick **0.34** — even for a utility this is thin, with $26B/yr gross debt issuance feeding refinancing risk into a sticky long-end.
