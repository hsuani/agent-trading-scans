# Bear case round 1 — TLN

## Thesis (1 sentence)
At $352.88, TLN already prices flawless multi-year AI-power monetization (33.4x EV/EBITDA, 14.8x P/B, +46% TTM) on top of a post-bankruptcy IPP whose FY25 GAAP NI was -$219M and whose net debt nearly doubled to $6.12B (~10x normalized EBITDA), leaving asymmetric downside on any execution slip, FERC/PJM read-through, or power-curve mean reversion.

## Demand / market risks
- **AI-power thesis = single-name concentration**: TLN's premium is almost entirely the AWS/Susquehanna PPA and the prospect of further hyperscaler deals. Datacenter capex cycles are notoriously procyclical; any Microsoft/Meta/AWS AI-capex pause repriices the entire nuclear-IPP cohort (already saw cohort sell-off on 5/07 despite strong VST print).
- **PJM BYOG / Two-Strike overhang**: FERC's May 18 compliance refiling and the PJM Two-Strike reliability regime (120-day pause / termination on 2nd violation) is a structural overhang for the colocation cohort — even if Talen's front-of-meter pivot insulates Susquehanna, *future* deal economics (SMR/AWS expansion, Cumulus uprate) are now constrained.
- **Power-price mean reversion**: FY25 gross margin expansion (32%→38%) is power-curve driven; PJM capacity prices and spark spreads have cyclical histories. A reversion to FY23-24 normals collapses EBITDA back toward $500-600M.

## Margin / cashflow risks
- **Q4'25 normalized EBITDA -$130M and $511M SG&A spike** (vs ~$40M typical) signal non-trivial hedge/MTM derivative losses or M&A frictions — quality of FY25 OCF/FCF is suspect until 10-K clarifies.
- **Crane integration**: $3.4B+ debt step-up to fund Crane gas-plant acquisition adds gas-fleet commodity exposure to what bulls value as "scarce nuclear." Carbon/IRA policy reversal under any 2026+ admin reshuffle hits the gas portion of the fleet directly.
- **Capex understated**: $206M FY25 capex is light vs the buildout narrative — meaningful AWS Cumulus / Susquehanna uprate spend is yet to come. TTM FCF $1.39B is unlikely to persist once growth capex normalizes.

## Valuation risk
- Bear: 12x EV/EBITDA (still premium to IPP median 8-12x) × FY26 mid-guide Adj EBITDA $1.90B = EV $22.8B; less $6.1B net debt = $16.7B equity / 45.4M sh = **$367/sh** → roughly flat. Strip premium to peer median (10x): EV $19.0B → equity $283/sh, **-20%**.
- Worst (power-curve crack + FERC adverse + cohort de-rate to 7x): $1.6B EBITDA × 7x = $11.2B EV - $6.1B debt = $5.1B equity = **$112/sh, -68%**.
- Forward P/E 10.3x requires ~$34 EPS — a >$38 swing from FY25 -$4.79. Any miss vs that ramp is binary.

## Competitive / regulatory
- **Creditor/distressed float overhang**: emerged from Ch.11 in 2023; Rubric (10%+ holder, distressed-debt origin) trimmed $114M at $288-298 in June 2025 and has not bought back since — telegraphs further distribution into strength.
- **MFN Partners, W.H. Reaves, Fred Alger** are specialist holders; concentrated specialist ownership reverses violently on thesis cracks.
- **Constellation/Vistra/Public Power competition** for the remaining hyperscaler PPAs — TLN is no longer the only nuclear-AI optionality (Meta/Vistra 2.6 GW, MSFT/CEG TMI restart).
- No dividend, equity cushion thin ($1.09B book, P/B 14.8x), buybacks decelerated 95% YoY ($1.96B FY24 → $103M FY25) — capital return story has already peaked.
